Common Questions

Package FAQs

How are the savings worked out?
We compare the bundle price to what each service would cost booked as its own separate visit at our standard rates. Bundling genuinely saves you money because we only need one call-out, one visit and one setup to do everything, no smoke and mirrors, just real efficiency passed on to you.
Can I mix and match, or add extra services?
Yes, the three packages are a starting point. Let us know what your home actually needs when you get in touch and we'll build a fixed-price quote around it.

The prices above are estimates for an average single-storey home. Double-storey or larger properties are quoted individually, always confirmed upfront before we start, no surprises on the day.
How long does a full package take?
Most Spring Shine and The Big Three jobs are done in one visit, same day. We'll confirm timing when we quote based on your property.

Spring is our busiest booking period, so slots do fill up. Get in early if you want a specific week.
